The beginning of 2021 saw a vast exodus from cities across the United States. This was especially true on Long Island, where the housing market was through the roof. In fact, houses in my neighborhood of Greenport Village were selling at market price in a matter of days after being placed for sale. This spike in the housing market, in combination with the success of housing rentals inspired me to design a possible rental house. During spring of 2021, I taught myself how to use Sweet Home 3d, a home design software, and modeled two rental houses using the architectural knowledge I gain from a class I took the previous fall.
