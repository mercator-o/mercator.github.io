This Yale-inspired coffee table combines a strong, stable design with an aesthetically appeasing appearance. Using SOLIDWORKS's force analysis software, this table was designed to hold a maximum of 500 pounds distributed in a one square foot area in the center of the tabletop. The table's construction material is balsa wood.
