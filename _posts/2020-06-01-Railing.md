During this past summer while at home taking online classes, I constructed a new modern wire railing system prompted by the rotting slats and posts of the previous deck railing. I put in all new 4x4 posts and used a wide 1x6 composite board for the top rail. Facing exterior to the deck, I placed a series of solar powered light that illuminate the deck at night.
