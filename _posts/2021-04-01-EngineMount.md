In my Computer Aided Design class, we were tasked with designing and testing a motor mount for an ultralight aircraft as shown on the left. The requirements of our design were that our motor mount had to be made only of "off the shelf" parts, withstand a "hard" landing, minimize weight, and secure the motor in the correct position (the blade had to be roughly 12" off the ground).

I first modeled a collection of quarter and half inch thick 6061 aluminum plates and rectangular tubing that I then modified to create the engine mount shown below. I next used Solidwork's study features to test the strength of my design and found that it met the requirements detailed above.
