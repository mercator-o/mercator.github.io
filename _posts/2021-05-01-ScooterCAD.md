My final project for my Computer Aided Design class consisted of improving upon my current design of my electric scooter (click below to see that project). Using SolidWorks, I modeled an improved folding mechanism and deck and created a more efficient drive system by integrating the rear wheel and sprocket. I then performed various stress tests -including static loadings, drop tests, bearing loadings- on the scooter to evaluate its design and durability.
