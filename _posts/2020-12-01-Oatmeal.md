In my Mechanical Design class, we were challenged with developing and building a mechanism inspired by a novel problem that arose due to COVID-19 pandemic. Due to the increase in restaurants closing, home cooked meals became more common. My team and I, therefore, came up with the idea of building an automatic oatmeal maker as a device that would ease the amount of cooking people had to do.

Over the course of the semester, we performed various calculations, analyzed various failure modes, and progressed through various design iterations, eventually resulting in the mechanism shown to the right.

While this project was a team effort, I specifically worked on creating the CAD models of the body, water system, button and electronics systems. My other major contribution was laser cutting all of the acrylics pieces for all of my team members.
