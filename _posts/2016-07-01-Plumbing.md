In order to grow oysters, you must not only need to know how to be a farmer, but also an electrician, mechanic, and a plumber. The photograph above illustrates some of my earlier plumbing experience. Here, I am mocking up the inflow/overflow water pipe that is used to bring up salt water into the upweller (wooden box), which are nursery systems for juvenile oysters.

On the right, just this past winter break, I installed a new water softener and filtration system for our well water set up. The previous system, which had been in use for many years, had broken. The bids that we received to replace it, however, were highly priced and so I convinced by parents that I was capable of researching and installing a new system. So far, we have been leak free!
