Above shows a bookshelf that I made for my sister using mill-cut, wide board walnut planks gifted to us by a neighbor. The design of the bookshelf resembles the letter "S," the first letter in my sister's name. The vertical members, which are a special Ipe hardwood often used for dock decks, support the shelves with the use of a series of wooden dowels. The rough texture of the unfinished walnut and the vibrant, beet-colored Ipe wood is highlighted by a coating of linseed oil.
